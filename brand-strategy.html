Values / Brand Beliefs — LOCKED

  • Looking good is not for special occasions. She doesn't have "event clothes" and "house clothes." She just has clothes and they all look good. Monday, Saturday, doesn't matter.

Content Framework — REFINING

Building from the brand beliefs and Topicals model. Key principle: sell the FEELING, not the product. Connect every post to a moment, a desire, or a belief. Never just show product.

What we know works (from research):

  • Low production, single-take content outperforms polished content in 2026
  • Carousels get the most saves and DMs (conversion content)
  • Founder speaking to camera builds trust faster than anything
  • Customer photos/UGC is 5x more trusted than brand content
  • Price visible on content removes the biggest DM barrier
  • Comment-to-DM automation converts at 12-18%

What we know doesn't work (from our experience):

  • Pure product showcase with no context, feeling, or price
  • Content that gets views but no DMs or saves
  • Designed thumbnails on every Reel (makes grid look AI/templated)
  • No founder voice — Cherechi visible but silent
  • Zero customer content/social proof

Content formats in rotation (to be refined):

  • Runway — one outfit, five ways to wear it (being refined)
  • Shop and Chat — two people come in, pick random outfits for each other
  • This or That — words/vibes and we pick an outfit that matches
  • 60 Second Styling — put together a complete look in 60 seconds
  • Switch Up — one piece styled multiple ways
  • More formats TBD based on the brand feeling we're building

Key Findings

What the data tells us
Working
Posting frequency of 4-5x/week was maintained in May-June. The styling content is a reliable workhorse. The Content Ideas sheet shows 45 ideas were brainstormed with reference links, showing initiative in the planning phase. Lifestyle content (outdoor, brunch) consistently outperforms other organic content.
Concern
24% of all content is trends and memes. This gets views but does not generate DMs or sales. The brand is at risk of being perceived as an entertainment page rather than a fashion brand you buy from. TikTok is significantly underperforming Instagram with no optimization. The tracker has 9 sheets but only 1 is being used.
Critical
Only 1 out of 66 posts had a "DM to order" CTA. No prices appear on any posts. DMs are not being tracked. 3 sales in 3 months from the entire online presence. The content is not built to convert attention into revenue. The Follower Growth sheet is completely empty, meaning we do not have verified week-by-week growth data.

Expectations for August - October

Posting Frequency
4-5 posts per week on the feed. Daily Stories. Everything cross-posted to TikTok. 3x/week is not acceptable for a brand at our stage.
Content Framework
All content must follow the 7 content types in the framework: The Runway, Shop & Chat, The Switch Up, Dates with CO, Real Talk, Voice & Vibes, Spotted. No more random posting.
Prices on Everything
Every post that shows a product must include the piece name and price. On screen in videos. In the caption. No exceptions.
CTA on Everything
Every product post must have a clear CTA: "DM your size to order." No more posts that show clothes without telling people how to buy.
Weekly Reporting
Every Friday: posts published, saves, shares, DMs received, DMs converted to sales, follower count. Use the tracker. Fill in every column.
DM Response Time
Under 30 minutes during business hours. This alone could 3x our conversion rate.
Founder Visibility
The founder should appear in at least 40% of content. Not optional. Data shows founder presence drives better performance.
Review Cadence
Bi-weekly content review using the tracker data. Monthly check-in on strategy. Next formal review: end of October.

Review Meeting Guide

How to run the conversation

1. Start with her self-assessment. Let her talk first. Listen to how she sees her own performance. Don't interrupt. Take notes on where her perception matches the data and where it doesn't.

2. Share the data. Walk through the numbers calmly. This is not about punishment. It's about showing her what the data says so she can see it herself. Focus on the gaps: DM triggers, prices, founder visibility, TikTok.

3. Acknowledge what worked. She maintained posting frequency. She brainstormed 45 content ideas. She managed the launch content. Give credit where it's due.

4. Be direct about what needs to change. The content is not built to sell. That's the core issue. Everything else (prices, CTAs, tracking) flows from that one problem. She needs to shift from "content poster" to "content that makes money."

5. Present the framework. Show her the content framework. This is the system going forward. She doesn't have to figure out what to post anymore. The system tells her. Her job is to execute it well.

6. Ask what she needs. Maybe she needs training. Maybe she needs clearer direction. Maybe she needs better tools. This is a two-way conversation.

7. Set clear next steps. Content calendar for August due by [date]. Weekly reporting starts this Friday. First Runway video shoots this week. Prices on the last 15 posts updated by [date].
